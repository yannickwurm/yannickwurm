# Yannick Wurm

I’m the founder of **[Sensibee](https://sensibee.io/)**, a nature-tech company building automated, non-lethal insect monitoring systems using AI and computer vision.

I’m also Professor of Evolutionary Genomics at Queen Mary University of London.
I created [SequenceServer](https://sequenceserver.com) to make BLAST of private/unpublished datasets easy.
I've done a lot of work on evolution of insect societies - and supergenes in fire ants!

I'm a programme Director at ARIA, where I lead the [Engineering Ecosystem Resilience](http://engineeringecosystemresilience.org/) efforts. 

## Current focus

- Scaling automated insect monitoring across the UK and internationally
- Making biodiversity data more continuous, robust and actionable
- Developing practical AI and computer-vision tools for ecological monitoring
